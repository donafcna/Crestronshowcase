## Prompt maître de la façade
Create a completely original photorealistic luxury residential architecture photograph for an interactive smart-home website, wide 16:9 landscape, highest photographic detail. No reference image is supplied. Original Swiss alpine foothill villa at blue hour, premium architectural magazine photography, subtle believable warm landscape lighting, atmospheric deep blue sky, distant Jura ridge and misty meadow, silver birches, ferns and ornamental grasses, no palm trees, no lake or city skyline. Architecture: asymmetrical interlocking two-storey pavilions stepped along a gentle slope, pitched standing-seam zinc roofs with shallow angles, substantial split-face charcoal gneiss piers, pale brushed limestone terrace, vertical smoked larch soffits, bronze narrow window mullions. Entire property in frame, near-frontal perspective from a modest elevated garden viewpoint, foliage does not obscure windows. Nine distinct glazed room openings separated by opaque stone piers: four upper (salon, dining, kitchen, office), five lower (cinema, gym, entry, master, guest). Offsets, pavilion heights, gables and depth articulate massing. Warm 3000K interiors with refined furnishings. Closed bronze entrance gate and charcoal garage door at far left. Small reflecting swimming pool at far right. Sculpted terraced garden. No people, interface, panel, logos or text.
