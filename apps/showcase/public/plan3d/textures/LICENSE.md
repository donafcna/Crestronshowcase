# Texture sources

`oak-color.webp`, `oak-normal.webp`, `oak-roughness.webp` are resized/compressed derivatives of **Wood Floor**, Poly Haven: https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_floor

Source: https://api.polyhaven.com/files/wood_floor (1K JPG diffuse, OpenGL normal, roughness).
License: CC0 1.0, https://polyhaven.com/license . No runtime connection to Poly Haven.
WebP dimensions: diffuse 1024², normal and roughness 512². Total: 111,392 bytes.

Other material textures are generated locally by `interiors.js` and `landscape.js`.

`stone-color.webp`, `stone-normal.webp`, `stone-roughness.webp` are compressed derivatives of **Sandstone Blocks 08**, Poly Haven: https://polyhaven.com/a/sandstone_blocks_08

Source: https://api.polyhaven.com/files/sandstone_blocks_08 (1K diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness).
License: CC0 1.0, https://polyhaven.com/license . Diffuse 1024², normal and roughness 512²; served locally. Glazing and environment reflections are generated by `estate.js` without remote assets.
